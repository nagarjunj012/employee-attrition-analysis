# Findings

- 6 of the 18 sample employees have `attrition` marked `Yes`.
- The overall attrition rate in this sample is 33.33%.
- By department, Sales has the highest attrition rate at 50%.
- By length of service, the 1–2 year group has a 66.67% rate, while the 3+ year group has a 0% rate.

## Limitations

- The dataset contains only 18 synthetic records.
- These results describe this sample only; they do not establish why employees leave or predict what will happen in a real organization.
