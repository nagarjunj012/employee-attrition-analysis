# Employee attrition data dictionary

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `employee_id` | Fictional identifier for an employee |
| `department` | Department where the employee works |
| `job_role` | Fictional job role |
| `years_at_company` | Number of years at the company |
| `attrition` | Whether the employee left: `Yes` or `No` |

## Data note
All records are synthetic and were created for practice. They do not represent real employees or a real company.
