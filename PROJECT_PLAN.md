# Project plan

## Business question
How does employee attrition vary across departments, job roles, and length of service in our sample data?

## Planned analysis
- Count employees and attrition cases.
- Calculate the attrition rate.
- Compare attrition across departments and job roles.
- Explore attrition by length of service.
- Present the results with charts.

## Data note
This project will use synthetic sample data, not real employee records.

## Scope
This is a descriptive analysis. It will explore patterns in the sample and will not establish why employees leave or predict future attrition.
